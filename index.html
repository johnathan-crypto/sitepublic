Questions

Do I really pay nothing until I approve it?

Yes. You see the finished site first. If you don't want it, you owe nothing and we take it down.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site is yours. If you ever leave the care plan, we hand you everything.

I already have a Facebook page. Why do I need a website?

Most customers search Google, not Facebook. A website ranks in search, looks more established, and gives people a one-tap way to call you.

What about a domain name?

We help you pick one and set it up. Domains usually cost about $12–20 a year, paid directly to the registrar in your name.
