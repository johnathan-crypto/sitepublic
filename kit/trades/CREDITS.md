# Photo credits

All trade photos are from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) and used under the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/):
free for commercial use, no attribution required, modification allowed. We credit photographers anyway.
The license does not allow selling unaltered copies or implying endorsement by people shown, so photos are only used
as generic trade imagery and never captioned as the client's own staff or jobs.

Files are cropped and converted to WebP by the build prep (`hero` 3:2 at 1600/960w plus a 4:5 mobile crop at 900w,
`about` 4:5 at 1000/600w, `s1`-`s3` 4:3 at 1200/600w). Photographer names were checked against the Pexels photo page
(or the file's embedded EXIF artist). Source URLs use the stable `pexels.com/photo/<id>/` form.

Unsplash was not used: unsplash.com and its API were not reachable from the build environment.
